## 1. The service

Aperture is software for governing spending by people and AI agents. It connects to accounts the Customer already has: AI providers, a card program, and blockchain wallets. It enforces budgets and policies on them, and records every decision in an audit log.

**Aperture never takes custody of Customer funds.**

- Card spending runs on the Customer's own card issuer account.
- Crypto spending runs from wallets the Customer owns. Aperture holds only a limited, revocable spending allowance that the Customer sets and can revoke at any time from its own wallet.

## 5. Limits of the controls

Aperture enforces budgets and policies on a best-effort, fail-closed basis:

- where it decides in real time (the gateway, card authorizations it answers, x402 payments it signs), a refusal prevents the spend;
- where it can only observe (usage a provider reports afterwards, card authorizations the issuer approves without asking Aperture, transfers made outside Aperture), it records and alerts.

The Customer is responsible for configuring provider-side and issuer-side limits as a backstop, as the product recommends.
